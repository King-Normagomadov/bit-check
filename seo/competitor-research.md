# Competitor Research — Bit-Check

## Primary Local Competitors

### BayCIX
- Strong established local authority
- Strong Landshut presence
- Local case studies and trust signals
- More enterprise/corporate positioning
- Benchmark for long-term local authority

### Lehrmann IT-Systemhaus
- Long-established Landshut provider
- Broad IT services
- Strong regional presence
- Less focused/specialized positioning

### Nexalogic IT Solutions
- Modern small-business positioning
- Strong service structure
- Clear conversion focus
- Transparent pricing
- Good benchmark for SMB-focused UX

### Pragmat IT
- Modern MSP positioning
- Strong managed IT / security focus
- Good regional targeting
- Strong benchmark for service packaging and compliance content

### FAVORIT.network
- Strong local landing-page structure
- Clear local targeting
- Good reviews/trust presentation
- Useful benchmark for local SEO page structure

## Main Competitive Opportunities for Bit-Check

1. Focus specifically on small and medium-sized businesses.
2. Build dedicated service pages instead of relying mainly on one broad page.
3. Build a strong Landshut-specific landing page.
4. Make services easy to understand for non-technical business owners.
5. Show real local proof: customers, projects, testimonials and results.
6. Build stronger content around Microsoft 365, IT support, backup and security.
7. Keep the website focused on business IT and avoid unrelated services.
