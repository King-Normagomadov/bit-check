### Keyword Clusters

**Homepage**

* Target: IT Dienstleister Landshut, IT Systemhaus Landshut, IT Service Landshut
* Focus: Establish primary local relevance and broad B2B authority.

**Managed IT Page**

* Target: Managed IT Landshut, IT Betreuung Unternehmen, proaktive IT-Betreuung
* Focus: Ongoing operational support, predictable IT costs, and proactive monitoring.

**Microsoft 365 / Cloud Page**

* Target: Microsoft 365 Betreuung Unternehmen, Microsoft 365 Migration, Cloud IT Lösungen
* Focus: Business transitions to the cloud, licensing, and administration (avoiding generic "Microsoft 365 Landshut" due to university dominance).



**Infrastructure & Network Page**

* Target: IT Infrastruktur Landshut, Netzwerkbetreuung Landshut, WLAN Unternehmen
* Focus: Server setups, structured networking, and Wi-Fi architecture.

**Backup & Continuity Page**

* Target: Datensicherung Unternehmen, Backup Landshut, Datenwiederherstellung
* Focus: Disaster recovery, Veeam backups, and business continuity.

**IT Consulting / Health Check Page**

* Target: IT Beratung Landshut, IT Check Unternehmen, IT Audit
* Focus: Strategic technology planning, vulnerability assessments, and digital funding (Digitalbonus).


### Keywords to Avoid for Now

* **IT Support Landshut:** Search algorithms heavily skew this toward job seekers and employment portals. Use "IT Betreuung" for B2B targets instead.


* **Microsoft 365 Landshut:** Saturated with university documentation and student login pages.


* **PC Reparatur Landshut / Laptop Reparatur:** Too consumer-focused (B2C) and implies reactive break-fix work rather than proactive B2B managed services.
* **Broad short-tail terms (e.g., "IT", "Netzwerk", "Cloud"):** Zero local relevance and impossible to rank for against international software vendors.
